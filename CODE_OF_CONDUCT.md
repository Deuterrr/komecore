# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and inclusive community, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in KomeCore a harassment-free and empowering experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive, collaborative environment include:

* **Using welcoming and inclusive language**: Being encouraging, constructive, and considerate in communications.
* **Respecting diverse viewpoints and experiences**: Listening openly to different perspectives and technical approaches.
* **Gracefully accepting constructive feedback**: Treating critique of code or documentation as an opportunity to learn and improve.
* **Focusing on what is best for the community**: Prioritizing collective software quality, security, and project health over individual preferences.
* **Showing empathy toward other members**: Being patient with newcomers, answering questions kindly, and offering assistance when possible.

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

* **Harassment in any form**: Public or private harassment, stalking, unwelcome attention, or sustained disruption of discussions.
* **Discriminatory or offensive remarks**: Trolling, derogatory comments, political or religious attacks, or personal insults.
* **Inappropriate imagery or language**: The use of sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention and advances.
* **Doxxing and privacy violations**: Publishing private personal information (such as physical addresses, email addresses, or phone numbers) without explicit, verifiable consent.
* **Unprofessional conduct**: Any behavior that could reasonably be considered harmful, abusive, or unprofessional in a software development collaboration.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They are expected to take appropriate, fair, and prompt corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers retain the right and responsibility to edit, redact, or delete comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently suspend any contributor whose actions are threatening, abusive, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all official KomeCore project spaces—including the GitHub repository, issue tracker, pull requests, and discussion forums—as well as in public spaces whenever an individual represents KomeCore or its community (e.g., using an official project email, speaking at conferences, or communicating via official social accounts).
